Email support@roamfly.app — we usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

  • A flight isn't found. Check the flight number and the departure date (local to the departure airport). Very new or charter flights may not be listed yet.
  • Times don't update. Live updates and alerts are part of Premium. Free accounts show the schedule.
  • Restoring a purchase. Profile → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.
  • Sharing a flight. On a flight, tap Share flight and send the link. Anyone with it sees that flight's status until 24 hours after it lands; tap Stop sharing to turn it off sooner.
  • Deleting your account. Profile → Delete account removes your account and all your data.
